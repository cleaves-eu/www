In short

RayNFC has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app itself sends nothing to me or to any server: what you read and save stays on your iPhone unless you share it yourself.

Tags, files and your library

The contents of the tags you scan, the files you open or import (from Files, AirDrop, Mail or a Flipper Zero) and what you add yourself (names, notes, tags, folders, icons, templates and Readers) are stored only on your iPhone, in RayNFC’s own storage. Saved tags and templates are also visible in the Files app (On My iPhone → RayNFC). Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you back up your iPhone (iCloud or a computer), iOS includes this data in the backup; Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.

Tags can hold personal data, also about other people, for example a contact card or a name on a card. You decide what you read, keep and share.

Reading tags (NFC)

RayNFC reads tags with the iPhone’s NFC reader through Apple’s Core NFC. A tag’s content goes from the tag into the app and nowhere else.

Flipper Zero (Bluetooth)

When you connect a Flipper Zero, RayNFC talks to it directly over Bluetooth to list and download its NFC files. Nothing goes over the internet. iOS asks for your permission to use Bluetooth first.

Sharing files

When you share a tag or a template (AirDrop, Mail, Messages, Files, …), it goes to the person or app you choose. Their privacy policy applies from there.

Contact cards

“Add to Contacts” opens Apple’s screen for a new contact. The contact is only saved if you tap Done there. RayNFC has no access to your contacts.

Links on tags

RayNFC asks before it opens a link stored on a tag. The link then opens in your browser or in the app it belongs to; that website’s or app’s privacy policy applies.

Reporting a bug

“Report a Bug” opens a new email in your mail app, with the app version, iOS version and iPhone model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. If you do, I process your email address and your message to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your message concerns your purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is answering you and fixing the bug. I delete the email once it’s no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods require me to keep it.

RayNFC Pro (in-app purchases)

Buying RayNFC Pro and the free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy. I don’t receive your name, Apple Account or payment details, only Apple’s sales reports, which contain no personal data. To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app asks Apple’s StoreKit on your iPhone.

Crash reports from Apple

If you’ve turned on “Share With App Developers” (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple shares crash reports and usage statistics of RayNFC with me, without your name. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can withdraw it at any time with that switch.

No sharing, no third countries, no automated decisions

Apart from what’s described above, I don’t share your data with anyone, I don’t sell it and I don’t transfer it to countries outside the EU. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). An informal message to the email address above is enough.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement.

Changes

I update this privacy policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.

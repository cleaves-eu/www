In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads nothing from other servers (no external fonts, scripts, videos or maps). Personal data is only processed in the cases described below.

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by netcup GmbH, Daimlerstraße 25, 76185 Karlsruhe, Germany, on servers in Germany. netcup processes the data on my behalf under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

Each time a page is requested, the server automatically records:

  • the IP address of the requesting device,
  • the date and time of the request,
  • the requested page or file, the HTTP status code and the amount of data transferred,
  • the previously visited page (referrer), if your browser sends it,
  • browser type and operating system (user agent).

This is needed to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable, for example to detect attacks and errors. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is this secure and stable operation. Without this data the website cannot be delivered. The log files are deleted after 7 days.

Language setting

The start page (www.cleaves.eu) reads your browser’s language setting to send you to the German or English version. This happens only in your browser; nothing is stored and nothing is sent to me.

Contact by email or phone

If you contact me by email or phone, I process the details you provide (for example your name, email address or phone number and the content of your message) to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request concerns a contract or steps prior to one, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is answering your request. I delete the data once it is no longer needed for this, unless statutory retention periods require me to keep it.

No sharing, no third countries, no automated decisions

Your data is not sold and is only shared with the host named above. It is not transferred to countries outside the EU. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). An informal message to the email address above is enough.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement.

Changes

I update this privacy policy when the website or the law changes. The version published here applies.
